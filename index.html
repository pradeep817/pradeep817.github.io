A decision I made

One shared core for two bank apps

Situation
Two regional bank apps, Kagb and Kgb, needed the same login, device binding and network setup.
Decision
Instead of building that twice, I designed one core package both apps depend on, with app-specific code kept outside it.
Result
Less duplicated setup, one standard infrastructure across both codebases, and fixes made once reach both apps.
